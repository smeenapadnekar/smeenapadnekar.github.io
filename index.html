About Me

Hello! I'm S Meena Padnekar, a PhD Research Scholar at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, working under the guidance of Prof. Sukhendu Das and Prof. Kaushik Mitra, and specializing in Computer Vision and Scene Understanding. My work focuses on learning semantic and geometric structure under limited supervision, particularly through multimodal perception, unsupervised segmentation, sparse-view 3D reconstruction, and 4D vision. I am broadly interested in developing robust visual learning methods for challenging real-world conditions.

My strengths lie in combining strong research thinking with hands-on technical execution, with experience in designing models, building training pipelines, running large-scale experiments, and translating ideas into end-to-end research systems.

Outside of coding and research, I enjoy exploring new technologies, building personal projects, reading widely, and having conversations about both technical and non-technical topics. I also enjoy travelling and experiences that bring new perspectives.
